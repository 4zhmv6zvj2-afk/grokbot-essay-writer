## stage_tool
When a copied sequence opens on stage 1, stages 1–3 run again. If the parent already saved stage 2 or 3, name the prior lock: stage number, stage title, and the parent sequence id. Ask whether to revise it or keep it. Show the Confirm card only when they revise or explicitly re-lock. Keep continues with the saved lock and does not show Confirm again for that same save. Essay: essay.stage. Takes a sequence id, confirmPhase, and stage 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, or 10. Draft writes tracking/<stage>.md from the stage rules and shows a Confirm card. Confirm advances tracking/stage.json only with cardAction confirm and cardDigest from that card. A typed ok, yes, or confirm saves only when the card cannot be used. A saved stage, or a later current stage, is refused. Not yet does not save. Stage 5 stores on the draft and starts stage 6 with no Confirm card. The reply started.stage is 6, with master_ask. The tool does not submit. A changed look needs a fresh draft. encourageChoice is the Stage 3 answer or skip. On a copied sequence, stage 4a takes paperChoice keep, drop, or replace. A drop leaves the list only. The file stays. A kept paper with a critical note is not gathered again.
