## writer_highest_band
When a mark scheme is present, the draft aims at the highest band, the top descriptors, not merely a pass.

## first_draft_note
The first draft (stage 5) is the report or the essay the brief asked for. That draft is the essay argument. It answers the question. It states the purpose, the argument, and the evidence inside that argument. The stage 4b critical-review markdowns stay source material. Do not paste them in as the body. One markdown per paper. Pass critical_reviews on the pack sent to essay.pack.validate. A section that only retells one paper and does not answer the question fails the draft check. Mark-scheme section titles are the headings when the mark scheme lists them. Re-open a source paper only with reopen when that markdown does not cover the gap. A quotation must be an exact span of that markdown, or of the re-opened passage. invent-forbid still holds. Confirm still holds: draft, show the human, then the same arguments. Stage 5 chat is a quiet step, the same idea as the later quiet steps. When quiet is true, say the chat line. If that reply also includes a status done line for the finished step, say that line too. Do not say that the step is starting on the turn that finishes it. Do not read rules, a file path, or the checklist. The person sees the finished first draft after the Word save. The Word preview and revise stay quiet unless the draft is blocked. When the 4b markdowns fill the stage 5 window, trim only the repeated model line, then split into whole-paper groups, then reopen with an exact passage. Do not silently truncate. invent-forbid still checks the full markdown. Keep the revise loop while a clear path remains. Stop and tell the person only when trim, split, and reopen are all exhausted. A paper stays in the body only when it supports the student's own sentence. Ask before adding a paper the student did not ask for. A rejected citation stays out when a swap does not succeed. When a swap does not succeed, offer the gap; do not put the rejected paper back. When the student supplied an outline or base prose, that outline is the structure: keep its headings and their order, and do not add a section or grow a paragraph around whatever was gathered. When the student asks for the cites to be shortened to about one or two sentences, write each cited point that short instead of leaving a longer stretch to be trimmed by hand.
